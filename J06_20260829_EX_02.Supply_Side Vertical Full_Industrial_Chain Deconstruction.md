## Vertical Full-Industrial-Chain Deconstruction
### I. Upstream Basic Materials & Components£ü-- Smooth Cyclical Stability
Assets take the form of general-purpose chemical consumables without rigid depreciation for dedicated production lines. AI is merely one downstream semiconductor sub-segment and cannot dictate overall industry cycles. The segment fluctuates moderately alongside conventional semiconductor cycles and only moderately drives high-end-material technical iteration. There exists no foundation for large-scale asset impairment or corporate elimination.

Triangular-criterion auxiliary assessment: moderate dedicated-lock-in degree, diversified business mix, controllable debt pressure; solid cyclical foundation.

### II. Semiconductor Equipment£ü-- R&D-Ecosystem-Driven Stability
Core assets consist of long-term technology R&D, process accumulation and customer-validation ecosystems, rather than plant-based hard heavy-assets with continuous rigid depreciation. The sector as a whole traverses cycles stably; only niche specialized-equipment sub-segments see individual-player elimination. Leading vendors face no existential threats or system-wide impairment.

Triangular-criterion auxiliary assessment: high technology lock-in, yet assets are reusable free from sunk-cost shackles; strong anti-volatility capability.

### III. Mid-Stream Manufacturing (Wafer / Storage / HBM)£ü-- Fatal Impairment & Priority Shake-Out
This segment bears the highest risk across the whole chain with extreme dedicated-hard-heavy-asset properties. Storage and HBM lines feature process-specific stacking, customer-specific configuration, complete non-repurposability, minimal residual value and perpetual rigid depreciation. After the 2H2027 capital inflection-point arrives, fab-utilization rates decline, unit-costs rise rigidly, product prices collapse, triggering priority cash-flow exhaustion, net-asset erosion and capacity shake-out.

#### Final Characterization of HBM (V1.6 Permanently Locked)
HBM possesses no endogenous long-term boom; it is defined as **a phased premium pool at the tail-end of the AI boom and is removed from long-term cyclical-profit recipient pools.**
1. Demand side: Heavy reliance on capital-funded training expenditures instead of steady-state end-user demand; massive inference workloads shift toward DDR-based architectures, driving sustained demand contraction.
2. Supply side: Low process-entry barriers and homogeneous product structures; three oligarchs simultaneously execute heavy-asset capacity expansion, creating pure capacity-driven cut-throat competition.
3. Stock-evolution side: DRAM process iteration steadily reduces stacking-layer counts and particle consumption per unit of computing power, compressing internal sector demand.

Cyclical conclusion: HBM will undergo priority impairment, price collapse and capacity elimination at the 2028 pivot and does not sustain long-run excess profits.

### IV. EDA / IP£ü-- Highest-Stability Segment Across the Chain (Risk-Boundary Final Completion)
Pure R&D-intensive lightweight assets with deep ecosystem lock-in; no fixed-asset depreciation or heavy-asset commitments. EDA licensing maintains high continuity and IP royalty-streams persist. Industry downturns only reduce new-order volumes, bringing no cyclical impairment or operational crises. This represents the most stable segment in the whole chain.

#### Strict Risk-Boundary Distinction (Finalized V1.6)
EDA/IP bears no cyclical-industry impairment risk. Geopolitical sanctions create policy-driven supply-interruption risks, which are external institutional risks distinct from cyclical-asset-volatility risks. The two categories must be strictly separated and do not negate its cross-cycle stable nature.

### V. AI Chip GPU/ASIC Design£ü-- Soft-Heavy-Asset Self-Reinforced Impairment (Chronological-Gap Final Completion)
No plant-based hard-assets or rigid depreciation. Risks originate from boom-driven self-reinforcing loops: ultra-high-boom profits ¡ú massive cash-flow for upfront commitments ¡ú next-generation-architecture R&D, upstream long-term capacity reservations, global ecosystem expansion, large-scale team build-up ¡ú build-up of huge intangible operating heavy-assets.

Cyclical-reversal logic: When capital expansion halts and procurement demand recedes, all pre-reserved capacity, pre-committed R&D and organizational-scale-up become impaired assets, bringing industry-scale profit draw-downs and massive write-down losses. Legal entities do not go bankrupt, yet business models, investment cadence and profit structures are fundamentally reshaped.

#### Precisely Finalized Chronological Gap
1. Manufacturing segment (Storage / HBM): Continuous bleeding from rigid depreciation ¡ú acute cash-flow breakdown; earliest failures and earliest shake-outs.
2. Design segment (GPU): Sunk-impairment of soft upfront assets ¡ú chronic profit avalanche; severe damage occurs with a lag.

Cyclical shock rule: **Manufacturing suffers first; design-segment damage arrives later.**

### VI. Advanced Intermediate Packaging / Traditional Packaging & Testing£ü-- Relatively General-Purpose & Moderate-Cutthroat Competition (Boundary Final Lock-In)
Assets qualify as semi-heavy-assets with partial reusability. Reusability is substantially higher than HBM / dedicated-storage production lines, supporting ASIC, Chiplet and optical-interconnect architectures for multi-scenario adaptation. Still, intermediate-packaging workflows are highly matched to GPU-HBM combinations, so systemic HBM-demand contraction transmits correlated supply-demand pressure.

Final conclusion: Cut-throat competition, profit compression and landscape consolidation occur, yet no system-wide industrial elimination. Stability exceeds manufacturing but lags behind tool-and-material segments.

### VII. Complete AI Servers£ü-- Business-Model Iteration & Restructuring
Assembly-integration lightweight assets with no rigid sunk costs. Conventional standardized-server sales face extreme cut-throat competition, low ceiling and long-term value erosion. Future core competition hinges on hardware carriers plus specialized models plus complete industry-oriented turnkey solutions; new-model offerings displace pure hardware-trading business-models. Corporations face no existential threats; only legacy-business-asset values keep eroding via iteration.

### VIII. Computing-Power-Electric-Grid Synergy Infrastructure (Datacenters / Cooling / Power Supply)£ü-- Unique Anti-Impairment Dividend Across the Whole Chain
Computing-power-base assets feature high generality, decoupled from AI, training-only workloads or any single chip architecture. They freely accept workload migration covering AI computing, general-purpose computing and data storage. Unique trait for this segment: no impairment amid cyclical downturns. As industries contract, it absorbs overflow workloads and improves asset-utilization ratios and returns, making it the primary steady-state-profit recipient after the 2029 landscape reshaping.

### IX. Four-Dimensional Chronological Outcomes for Full Chain (2026-2029)
#### 2026 Late-Boom Phase

#### 2H2027 Expectation Correction

#### 2028 Cycle Inflection Pivot (Whole-Chain Survival-Failure Screening ¡¤ Core Year)
